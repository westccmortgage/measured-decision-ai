The platform thesis

What was built.
What it cost.
What supports the claim.

Large projects produce photographs, scans, schedules, invoices, BIM models, draw requests, change orders, inspections, and professional observations. The information exists—but the relationships between physical progress, supporting evidence, and capital decisions are still reconstructed manually.

Measured Decision AI is building a governed evidence layer that connects those records without hiding their source, status, or limitations.

02 · Records

03 · Capital

The reviewer rebuilds the story.

    Receive the request

  2. 02

    Create the capture mission

    Translate the review scope into defined locations, viewpoints, components, and repeatable capture tasks.

  3. 03

    Build the spatial record

    Ingest phone, 360, LiDAR, fixed-camera, or drone data while preserving every original source.

  4. 04

    Compare and reconcile

    Associate visible change and project records with the relevant schedule activity and financial event.

  5. 05

    Surface exceptions

    Separate what is supported from what is missing, conflicting, inferred, or outside the capture.

  6. 06

    Complete human review

    Give the responsible professional a traceable record for follow-up and final disposition.

The platform supports evidence review. It does not replace inspections, certify code compliance, determine construction quality, or independently authorize the release of funds.

Responsible intelligence

Clear about the evidence.
Clear about the uncertainty.

The system must never make organization look like verification. Every material statement is assigned an evidence state that remains visible to the reviewer.

Observed

Visible in a dated capture, without extending the claim beyond what the sensor can show.

Calculated

Produced by defined, reproducible logic with identifiable inputs and formulas.

Verified

Confirmed through the required professional, documentary, or procedural review.

Inferred

Suggested by AI or pattern analysis and clearly separated from confirmed fact.

Missing

Required context or evidence has not been supplied or captured.

Conflicting

Sources disagree and the inconsistency must be resolved by a responsible person.

Measured Decision AI is developing decision-support and evidence-management technology. The platform does not replace licensed inspection, professional certification, underwriting, legal review, or the human responsibility behind a consequential outcome.
