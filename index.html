From observation to decision

What happened is measurable.
What happens next is a decision.

The system creates a continuous evidence layer around a physical environment and turns change into structured, reviewable decision support.

  1. 01 · Observe

    Capture what is actually happening.

    Video, images, spatial capture, documents, sensors, timestamps, and human observations.

  2. 02 · Measure

    Understand what changed.

    Compare current conditions with plans, prior states, requirements, schedules, or expected progress.

  3. 03 · Understand

    Connect evidence and consequences.

    Surface discrepancies, missing information, progress, risk, and downstream impact.

  4. 04 · Decide

    Present the next decision clearly.

    Recommendations, alternatives, supporting evidence, uncertainty, and human approval remain visible.
