  2. 02 · Platform

Company

Domain experience inside the problem.

Measured Decision AI grew from more than two decades of hands-on experience across mortgage finance, real estate, construction, and capital decisions—and from a recurring problem: important decisions are often made only after people manually reconstruct the evidence behind them.

We are building a governed spatial-evidence platform beginning with construction capital verification, while preserving a clear path toward insurance, claims, asset management, maintenance, and due diligence.
