          Start here · Capture guide

          Turn a job site into a room-by-room evidence record.

          The Studio tells the capture team what to record, where each file belongs, what supporting proof is missing, and what will later be available inside the spatial review experience.

          The organizing rule

          Property → Building → Level → Room → Component → Evidence

          Never upload an unexplained file. Every source needs a physical location, a subject, an evidence type, and a reason it matters.

          01

          Build the space map

          Create the buildings, floors, rooms, exterior zones, or commercial work areas before uploading files.

          Example: Main House → Level 1 → Formal Living
          02

          Capture the overview

          Record one slow 360 capture or walkthrough that establishes the full room and its orientation.

          Stand near the center; keep doors, windows, walls, floor, and ceiling visible.
          03

          Capture important details

          Add close photos or short clips for windows, doors, wiring, plumbing, finishes, equipment, defects, and concealed work.

          Name the exact subject: “North window W-03,” not “window video.”
          04

          Attach the proof

          Upload plans, invoices, product labels, installation records, inspections, warranties, and change orders to the same room or component.

          Example: W-03 detail photo + approved window schedule + installation invoice.
          05

          Review the gaps

          The system separates what is visible from what is merely claimed and asks for missing angles, dates, documents, or professional verification.

          A 360 view provides context; it does not prove what is behind a finished wall.
          06

          Prepare the spatial package

          Only organized and reviewed evidence is selected for the Vision Pro manifest. Original files remain private and traceable.

          Current: governed record and manifest. Next: native spatial viewer and anchored room navigation.

          What to upload

          One room, four evidence layers

          01 · Orientation360 room capture or complete walkthrough

          Answers: Where is everything?

          02 · DetailClose photos and short component videos

          Answers: What exactly is visible?

          03 · Documentary proofPlans, invoices, inspections, labels, warranties

          Answers: What supports the claim?

          04 · Time and changeRepeat captures at important construction stages

          Answers: What changed, and when?

          Target Vision Pro review

          How the reviewer will use the record.

          1. 01

            Select the property and date. Open the correct governed release—not a loose folder of files.

          2. 02

            Enter a building, floor, and room. Navigate through the same structure created during intake.

          3. 03

            Look at a component and select its marker. See the close-up, document, date, source, and review status together.

          4. 04

            Compare stages and record a disposition. Review change over time while keeping the final decision human.

          Product status: Studio intake and governed manifest are the current foundation. The native Vision Pro viewing experience is the next development layer and is not presented as released functionality.
