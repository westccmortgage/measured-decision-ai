Measured Decision AI · Investor Overview

The physical world has an evidence problem.

Buildings change in the real world. The photographs, plans, invoices, inspections, and financial decisions around them remain scattered. We are building the governed evidence layer that connects them.

The opportunity

Physical operations are becoming measurable.

Organizations already collect cameras, plans, schedules, records, and sensor data. The missing layer is a governed system that connects what changed, what supports it, what is affected, and what requires a human decision.

01

Spatial capture

Phone, 360°, video, drone, LiDAR, documents, and approved enterprise sources enter one evidence record.

02

Temporal intelligence

AI organizes change over time and prepares supported observations, discrepancies, and missing information.

03

Consequential workflows

Construction, film production, facilities, insurance, and other physical operations need reviewable evidence before action.

04

Human accountability

Sources, uncertainty, alternatives, and human confirmation remain visible instead of disappearing inside a black box.

Related-party disclosure · Construction pilot

The construction pilot operates on a real property using real captured data. The project is affiliated with the founder rather than an independent third-party paying customer. It is presented as evidence that the system operates in real field conditions—not as evidence of independent commercial traction.

Current commercial status

As of the date of this page, Measured Decision AI has no independent paying customers and has not generated revenue from independent customers. The operational Studio, evidence intake, storage layer, multi-agent architecture, and construction pilot have been built and operated with capital contributed by the founder.

We state this plainly because it is the correct baseline for understanding the company: what exists today was built without outside funding and without a customer paying us to build it.
