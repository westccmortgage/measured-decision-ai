One change. Connected consequences.

A production problem becomes a reviewable decision.

The system does not run the production. It organizes the changing facts, exposes downstream impact, and gives authorized people a clearer basis for action.

  1. 01Observe the change

    A location, set condition, weather event, crew issue, equipment conflict, or continuity concern is recorded.

  2. 02Connect the evidence

    Call sheets, schedules, scene requirements, crew, equipment, locations, prior capture, and timestamps are linked.

  3. 03Measure the impact

    Specialized agents compare dependencies and surface conflicts, missing facts, delay risk, and affected work.

  4. 04Present the options

    Alternatives, tradeoffs, source evidence, and uncertainty appear together for production leadership.

  5. 05Human decides

    The producer, AD, department head, or other authorized person retains responsibility for the next action.

Multi-agent production intelligence

Different questions require different specialists.

Observe

What changed?

Conditions, activity, visible differences, timing, and location.

Evidence

What supports it?

Capture, call sheets, schedules, reports, source records, and continuity references.

Compare

What is affected?

Scenes, crew, equipment, departments, dependencies, and downstream work.

Risk

What could follow?

Conflicts, missing information, delays, resource pressure, and operational exposure.

Decide

What are the options?

Reviewable alternatives and explanations—not an automatic final decision.
